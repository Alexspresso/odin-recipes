Fried Rice

Description

Originating in China's Sui dynasty, fried rice is a popular asian dish consisting of rice, egg, vegetables, aromatics, and a meat or seafood of your choice if you desire. Day old rice is ideal for fried rice, as it is dry and easily separated, though fresh rice can also be used.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked long-grain rice(ideally day-old, leftover rice)
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 cup white onion, diced
  • 1 cup peas and carrots (fresh or frozen)
  • 2-3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons green onions, chopped
