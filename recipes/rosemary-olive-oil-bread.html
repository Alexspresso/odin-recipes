Rosemary and Olive Oil Bread

Description

Rosemary and olive oil breads is a classic rustic-style loaf of bread with a crispy crust and a soft, chewy inside. The herbs and olive oil give a fresh, earthy flavor that pairs well with a simple butter and is excellent as a sandwich bread. Much like a lot of baking, this recipe's ingredients are best measured by weight (grams), though the volume measurements are included when available for those who do not have or can't be bothered with a scale (valid)

Ingredients

  • 360g (3 cups) unbleached AP flour
  • 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 8g (1 1/4 teaspoons) salt
  • 67g (1/3 cup) olive oil
  • 170g (3/4 cup) water, plus more as needed
  • 1-2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped.

Steps

  1. Weigh flour (or measure by gently spooning flour into measuring cup and sweep off excess)
  2. Combine flour, yeast, and salt in stand mixer with a dough hook attachment. Add olive oil and 3/4 cup of water as the mixer runs.
  3. Allow mixer to run until the mixture forms a slightly sticky ball clearing the sides of the mixer. If it appears too dry, add water one teaspoon at a time until the slightly sticky ball forms.
  4. Turn dough onto lightly floured surface. Dust hands lightly with flour, then knead rosemary into the dough until smooth.
  5. Place in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 2 hours.
  6. After the first rise, deflate the ball and shape it into a boule. Flour a clean kitchen towel and use it to line a bowl (or use a well-flowered brotform), and place the boule in it, seam side up. Let rise for 1-2 hours until puffy.
  7. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C) when dough is nearly done rising. Place cast iron skillet on the lowest rack, and a baking stone on the rack above it.
  8. Turn fully risen dough out onto a peel/parchment paper lined baking sheet, dust with flour, slash the top with a sharp knife.
  9. Slide the loaf onto a baking stone.
  10. Pour 1 cup water into the cast iron skillet to create steam. Bake bread for 45-50 minutes.
  11. Remove from oven, cool on a wire rack. Once cooled, slice or store as desired.
