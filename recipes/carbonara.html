Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti carbonara is a traditional Italian pasta dish from Rome made with egg, hard cheese, guanciale, and pepper. The dish arrived at its modern form, with its current name, in the middle of the 20th century.

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • 4 large eggs, plus 2 large yolks
  • 1 cup freshly grated parmesean, plus more for sprinkling
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated pecorino romano
  • 1 tablespoon coarsely ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter at room temperature (optional)
  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 1 12-ounce piece of pancetta, cut into matchsticks

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil over high heat.
  2. Whisk together eggs, yolks, parmesean, pecorino, pepper, and butter (if using) in a large bowl until combined well. Set aside.
  3. Cook pasta per package instructions; reserve one (1) cup pasta cooking water, set aside. Drain pasta.
  4. Meanwhile, cook pancetta in a large skillet over medium heat, stirring occasionally until crisp.
  5. Add cooked spaghetti and half the reserved pasta water to the skillet. Toss to combine.
  6. Add the egg mixture and toss to coat the spaghetti. Cook, tossing continuously to prevent the eggs from scrambling, until the sauce is creamy. If it becomes too thick, thin it out with a little of the pasta water until it is saucy, but not watery.
  7. Serve hot with more parmesean sprinkled on top.
